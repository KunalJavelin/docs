Administer your LangSmith organization: set up accounts and API keys, organize workspaces, manage users and access control, and configure shared context and platform tools.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account" icon="user-plus">
    Sign up at [smith.langchain.com](https://smith.langchain.com), then follow [Create an account and API key](/langsmith/create-account-api-key).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your profile" icon="settings">
    Set your preferences in [Profile configuration](/langsmith/profile-configuration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your organization" icon="users-group">
    Plan your hierarchy in [Account administration](/langsmith/administration-overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>
